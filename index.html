A little help

1. Pick your artwork. Import or drop an SVG, or expand the library. The original purple bloom has separate petals and stamens.

2. Select a part. Click the drawing or a layer. Expand groups to reach their children. Shift-click for multiple selection. Drag selected objects to move them.

3. Animate. Choose a suggestion or effect. Add more clips, drag timeline bars to move them, and drag their right edge to resize. Scrub to preview exact moments. Click a timeline clip, then press Delete or Backspace (or Remove clip) to remove only that animation. The artwork stays.

4. Style. Change fills, strokes, gradients, opacity, scale and rotation. Canvas controls handle dimensions and crop bounds.

5. Export. Download animated SVG, static SVG, a CSS/ SVG pair, a working HTML example or an editable project.

Arrange the workspace. Layers toggles the left panel. Clicking an already-active Animate, Colors, or Canvas tool closes the right inspector; click again to reopen it. Drag the divider above the timeline up or down to adjust its height. You can also focus the divider and use the arrow keys.

Sounds & support. The header speaker silently toggles the two approved recordings at 20% volume; your preference is saved. No sound plays on page load. The coffee-cup link opens optional project support on Ko-fi in a new tab.

Studio Artwork. Find all 81 new vector artworks with descriptive category names; hover to see the original collection names. Individual paths stay editable. Shift-click Group (or Ctrl/Command Shift G) to ungroup; first remove any animation applied to the parent group.

Selection toggles. Click Select all again to deselect everything, or click the stage margin outside the canvas frame. Clicking an active suggested animation removes that effect only; artwork and other clips remain.

Browse artwork. The expanded library keeps the left tool rail accessible. Choose Online · Iconify to browse automatically. Typing updates partial-name matches; Search or Enter runs immediately. Previous/Next pages adapt to the available space and skip broken icons.

Curved reveals. Choose Curved reveal for stroked curves, or draw a custom route through a filled band. Adjust brush width and drag route points. Coordinate curve timings can match the start/finish, sequence the curves or calculate durations from their lengths. Reveal direction also supports edges, corners and center-out options. Reveal from end starts at the opposite endpoint; Reverse plays the animation backward.

Morph & nodes. Morph supports matching path command structures. Nodes can be edited for M/L/C/Q/S/T/H/V commands. Arc paths remain selectable but are not node-editable.

Local files. Open index.html directly. Online icons need internet access. Save a project to keep a portable copy; browser autosave is best-effort. If a “Not saved” warning appears, download your project before closing the editor. Invalid project files leave your current canvas unchanged.
